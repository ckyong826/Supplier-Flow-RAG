# RCCB and RCBO overcurrent-protection reference

Reference facts for distinguishing residual-current protection from overcurrent protection.

- An RCCB does not include integrated overload or short-circuit protection. It must be coordinated in series with an MCB or fuse.
- An RCCB responds to residual or earth-leakage current; it does not replace the overcurrent device for overloads or line-to-neutral short circuits.
- An RCBO combines residual-current protection with the overcurrent protection normally provided by an MCB, subject to the product's rated limits.
- Do not answer an overload or short-circuit protection question with an RCCB's residual-current sensitivity.
