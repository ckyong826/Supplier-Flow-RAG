# TeSys D auxiliary-contact reference

Reference facts for reading the auxiliary-contact markings on Schneider TeSys D contactors.

Quick answer: for a TeSys D contactor with 1NO + 1NC, the auxiliary-contact terminal pair is NO 13 and 14, while NC is 21 and 22. The terminal pair identifies the NO and NC auxiliary contacts.

- A normally open auxiliary contact is identified by terminals 13 and 14.
- A normally closed auxiliary contact is identified by terminals 21 and 22.
- `NO` and `NC` describe the contact state when the contactor coil is de-energised; terminal numbers identify the connection points. They are different pieces of information.
- Always confirm the exact contact arrangement on the SKU's wiring diagram before installation. An answer that says `1NO + 1NC` does not by itself identify the terminal numbers.
