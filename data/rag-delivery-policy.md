# Delivery Policy

## Standard delivery

Klang Valley deliveries normally take 1–2 working days after order confirmation and stock allocation.

Peninsular Malaysia deliveries outside Klang Valley normally take 2–4 working days.

East Malaysia deliveries normally take 3–5 working days. Remote areas may require additional transit time.

## Cut-off and urgent deliveries

Orders confirmed before 2:00 PM on a working day are processed on the same day, subject to stock availability. Orders confirmed after the cut-off are processed on the next working day.

Urgent same-day delivery may be arranged in Klang Valley subject to vehicle availability. Delivery charges must be confirmed before quotation approval.

## Delivery confirmation

Delivery dates are estimates until the sales team confirms stock allocation. Split delivery may be proposed when part of the requested quantity is immediately available.
