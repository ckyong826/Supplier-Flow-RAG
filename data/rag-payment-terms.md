# Payment Terms

Quick answer: a standard quotation is valid for 14 calendar days from the quotation date unless stated otherwise.

Standard quotation validity is 14 calendar days from quotation date unless stated otherwise.

New customers require payment before delivery unless approved credit terms are confirmed in writing.

Approved account customers may receive 30-day credit terms subject to credit limit and account status.

Bank transfer payment references must include the quotation or invoice number. Prices exclude delivery charges unless delivery is specifically included in the quotation.

Project pricing and special-order items may require a deposit before the order is released to the manufacturer.
