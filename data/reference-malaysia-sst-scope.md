# Malaysia SST scope reference

Reference facts from the Royal Malaysian Customs Department's MySST portal. This is a scope note, not a quotation tax calculation.

Quick answer: Malaysia sales tax can apply to taxable goods manufactured in Malaysia and taxable goods imported into Malaysia; the scope note does not state one universal SST rate or percentage.

- Malaysia sales tax is described as a single-stage tax imposed on taxable goods manufactured in Malaysia by registered persons and sold by them, and on taxable goods imported into Malaysia.
- Service tax applies to taxable services provided by a taxable person in Malaysia in the course of business.
- The scope statement does not provide a universal SST percentage for every electrical product or quotation.
- For a quotation, confirm the applicable rate, exemption, classification, and effective order from the current official SST material; do not invent a rate from the word `SST` alone.

Source: https://mysst.customs.gov.my/
