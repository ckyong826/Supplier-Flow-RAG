# TeSys D DC-coil reference

Reference facts for wiring Schneider TeSys D contactors with DC control coils.

- Schneider states that the DC coil has a built-in suppression device, such as a bidirectional diode, on the applicable TeSys D versions.
- Because of the built-in suppressor, the DC coil is polarity sensitive: A1 is the positive terminal as marked on the contactor.
- This is a coil-wiring fact, not a guarantee about every TeSys family or every generation. Verify the exact SKU marking and wiring diagram.
