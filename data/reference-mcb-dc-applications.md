# Acti9 iC60 DC-application reference

Reference facts for checking whether an Acti9 iC60 MCB can be used on a DC supply.

- Schneider states that Acti9 iC60N/H MCBs can be used for DC applications from 12 VDC to 250 VDC.
- The usable voltage and achieved breaking capacity depend on the number of poles connected in series and the required breaking capacity.
- A statement that the family is suitable for DC does not establish the breaking capacity of every single-pole or multi-pole wiring arrangement. Check the device diagram and the applicable product documentation.

Source: https://www.se.com/au/en/faqs/FA272282/
