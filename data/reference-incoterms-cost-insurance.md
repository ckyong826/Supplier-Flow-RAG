# Incoterms 2020 cost and insurance reference

Reference facts from the ICC about selected Incoterms 2020 details. These are trade-term rules, not SupplierFlow-specific commercial promises.

- Incoterms 2020 places the costs associated with a rule in article A9/B9, while the individual rule articles provide the detailed obligations.
- CIF uses Institute Cargo Clauses (C) as the default insurance level; CIP requires a higher level of cover using Institute Cargo Clauses (A) or similar clauses.
- FCA was revised so the parties can agree that the buyer instructs the carrier to issue an on-board bill of lading to the seller after loading.
- These details do not state a SupplierFlow delivery time, shipping charge, tax rate, stock position, or credit limit.

Source: https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/incoterms-2020/
