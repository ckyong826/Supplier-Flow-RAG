# SPD electrical-characteristics reference

Reference definitions for common low-voltage surge-protective-device ratings.

Quick answer: for an SPD, Uc is maximum continuous operating voltage, Up is voltage protection level, In is nominal discharge current, and Imax is maximum discharge-current peak.

- Uc is the maximum continuous operating voltage that may be applied to the SPD.
- Up is the voltage protection level: the residual voltage associated with the SPD at its stated nominal discharge current.
- In is the nominal discharge current, normally specified with an 8/20 microsecond current wave for Type 2 SPD selection.
- Imax is the maximum discharge-current peak the SPD can handle for the specified test waveform and duty.
- Choosing a larger Imax alone does not prove lower equipment stress or better protection. Selection also checks Uc, Up, In, the SPD type, the installation system, and the coordinated short-circuit protective device.
