# TeSys AC utilization-category reference

Reference facts for selecting a contactor by load duty. These notes do not replace the exact product catalogue or application table.

Quick answer: an AC-3 rating must not be reused as an AC-1 rating without checking the load, selection table and any required de-rating or derating.

- AC-1 covers non-inductive or resistive loads, or only slightly inductive loads, such as heating and distribution loads. Schneider describes this category with power factor at least 0.95.
- AC-3 covers squirrel-cage motor duty, including starting and switching off while the motor is running.
- Contactor selection depends on the load, voltage, current or motor power, and operating cycle. The same contactor can have different allowable ratings under AC-1 and AC-3, so a rating must not be transferred between categories without checking the selection table or any required de-rating.
