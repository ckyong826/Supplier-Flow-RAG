# Incoterms 2020 reference

Reference facts from the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC). Incoterms are a trade-term standard; they are not SupplierFlow's own quotation, payment, delivery, or tax policy.

- Incoterms 2020 contains 11 Incoterms trade terms.
- The rules allocate responsibilities and costs between the buyer and seller for the agreed transaction. They help define who arranges parts of carriage, clearance, and insurance under the selected term.
- The agreed Incoterms rule does not by itself publish a SupplierFlow delivery time, shipping charge, SST rate, credit limit, or product price.
- The term must be read together with the named place and the commercial contract. Do not answer a customer-specific delivery question from the trade-term name alone.
- ICC notes that DPU replaced the former DAT name in the 2020 edition, and that CIF and CIP use different default insurance levels.

Source: https://iccwbo.org/business-solutions/incoterms-rules/incoterms-2020/
