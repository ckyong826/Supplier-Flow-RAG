# Sales Quotation SOP

1. Confirm customer company name, contact name, email, delivery location, requested quantity, required date, and preferred brand.

2. Search the product database for an exact SKU match. Verify technical specifications against the product record or datasheet notes.

3. If the requested brand is unavailable, present a technically suitable equivalent and mark it as an alternative in the quotation.

4. Use the product database for public prices. Confirm project pricing, discount, SST, delivery fee, and payment terms before sending.

5. Save the quotation as Draft for internal review. Only mark it Sent after sales approval.

6. Follow up a sent quotation within 3 working days. Record call outcomes and customer feedback in the RFQ activity log.

7. When the customer accepts, mark the quotation Accepted and the RFQ Won. When the customer declines, mark the quotation Rejected and the RFQ Lost.
