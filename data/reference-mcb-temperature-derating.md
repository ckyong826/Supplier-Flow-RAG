# MCB temperature and derating reference

Reference facts for interpreting Acti9 iC60 overload values. Exact derating still comes from the requested device table.

- Schneider's iC60 derating tables are based on IEC 60898-1 and IEC 60947-2 testing.
- Under IEC 60898-1, the reference temperature is 30°C, the conventional non-tripping current is 1.13 × In, and the conventional tripping current is 1.45 × In for one hour.
- Under IEC 60947-2, the conventional non-tripping current is 1.05 × In and the conventional tripping current is 1.3 × In for one hour.
- The manufacturer defines the ambient reference temperature. Schneider states 50°C for MCBs compliant with both standards and 40°C for MCBs compliant only with IEC 60947-2. Do not assume the printed rated current is unchanged at every ambient temperature.

Source: https://www.se.com/ca/en/faqs/FA369327/
