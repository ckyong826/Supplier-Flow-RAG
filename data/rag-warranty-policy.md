# Warranty Policy

Electrical products are covered by the manufacturer warranty where stated on the product listing or quotation.

LED panel lights carry a 3-year manufacturer warranty. LED floodlights carry a 2-year manufacturer warranty.

Warranty claims require the original invoice or quotation reference, clear product photographs, and a description of the fault.

Warranty does not cover incorrect installation, physical damage, water ingress beyond the stated IP rating, electrical surge damage, modification, or use outside published technical specifications.

The supplier may request the item to be returned for inspection before approving replacement or credit.
