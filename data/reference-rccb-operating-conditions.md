# ABB F200 operating-conditions reference

Reference facts for the ABB F200 RCCB product family. Family-level facts must not be substituted for an individual SKU specification.

- ABB states that F200 RCCBs can operate in ambient temperatures from -25°C to +55°C.
- The F200 family page lists rated currents from 16 A to 125 A and sensitivities from 10 mA to 1000 mA.
- The family includes a test button for checking correct device function.
- The exact current, residual-current type, sensitivity, poles, and voltage still belong to the individual product code.

Source: https://new.abb.com/low-voltage/products/system-pro-m/residual-current-devices/rccb/f200
