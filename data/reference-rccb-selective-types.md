# ABB selective-RCD type reference

Reference facts for distinguishing delayed and transient-resistant RCCB variants.

- Type S is a selective, time-delayed residual-current device intended to coordinate with downstream devices that trip faster.
- ABB describes AP-R as a short-time delayed, transient-resistant variant that helps avoid unwanted tripping from short transients.
- Type S delay is for coordination, not a substitute for additional protection of people. ABB says Type S is not intended for people-protection applications or circuits with sensitivity at or below 30 mA; it is generally used upstream with a higher sensitivity such as 100 mA or more.
- The exact suffix, delay, sensitivity, and application must still be checked against the individual product documentation.
