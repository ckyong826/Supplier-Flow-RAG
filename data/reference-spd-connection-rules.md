# SPD connection-length reference

Quick answer: do not ignore the 50 cm SPD connection rule just because the quoted Up is low; keep the connection conductors short and follow the installation guidance.

Reference installation rule for wiring a low-voltage surge-protective device.

- Keep the total connection length from the conductors to the SPD and back to the protective or equipotential connection as short as possible; Schneider's guide gives a maximum total length of 50 cm for the described arrangement.
- Long conductors add inductive voltage drop during a surge, so the effective protection level at the equipment can be higher than the SPD's quoted Up.
- A low quoted Up does not remove the need to follow the connection-length rule and the manufacturer's installation instructions.
