# Incoterms 2020 risk-transfer reference

Reference facts from the ICC for the relationship between named place, delivery, carriage, and risk. Incoterms are not SupplierFlow delivery-time promises.

- The named place or port attached to an Incoterms rule is critical because it identifies the delivery point and the point at which risk transfers, depending on the rule.
- For the C rules, the named place is the destination to which the seller arranges and pays for carriage; it is not the delivery point where risk transfers.
- Under CPT and CIP, risk can transfer when the goods are handed to the carrier even though the seller contracts carriage to the named destination.
- Always specify the rule, year, and named place or point precisely in the sale contract.
