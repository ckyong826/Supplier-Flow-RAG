# MCB short-circuit current-limiting reference

Reference facts for separating current limiting from a circuit breaker's rated breaking capacity.

- Current-limiting capacity reduces the amplitude of a short-circuit current and the dissipated power or thermal stress in the installation.
- Schneider describes limiting curves using prospective short-circuit current, limited peak current, and thermal stress.
- A current-limiting curve is not a replacement for the product's rated breaking capacity. Do not answer a kA breaking-capacity question with a limiting-curve value.
- Cable protection and discrimination still depend on the breaker, upstream devices, installation conditions, and the applicable coordination tables.
